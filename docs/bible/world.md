# World Bible

## Sonpahari (सोनपहाड़ी)

A fictional village in **Jharkhand**, surrounded by monsoon **sal forest**, beside a railway line. The road in is narrow and broken, and villagers avoid it after dark. The year is **2009**; the night of the disappearance was **17 August 1992**.

It should feel lived-in, neglected and believable. It's an Indian place, not a Western horror set with Indian props added.

## Authenticity checklist

Use these. Don't overuse them.
- Signage in Devanagari first, English second. Painted boards with rain streaks and flaking paint.
- 50 Hz mains: tube-light buzz, ceiling fans, voltage dips.
- Railway: broad gauge, concrete sleepers, ballast, lifting-barrier booms with a STOP disc, a gumti, colour-light signals, distant freight horns.
- Monsoon: puddles in road dips, leaf litter, wet laterite and mud, frogs and insects (sound), wires moving in the wind.
- Period, 2009: no smartphones in shot. A taxi like an Ambassador or Sumo. Cassettes are already old; the 1992 tape is an artefact.
- Interiors: lime-washed walls with a painted dado, calendars, framed photos with a garland, steel trunks.

## Avoid

Generic Western haunted-house design. Exaggerated decay. Random Indian props scattered for "flavour". Saturated red lighting. Bollywood horror clichés (screaming, a white-sari ghost floating through the air).
